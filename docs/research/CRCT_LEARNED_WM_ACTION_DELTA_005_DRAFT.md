## Why 004 did not answer the Level-3 question

004 asked whether CRCT recovered the computation implementing `ax → Δvx`,
or only the channel through which `ax` enters.

At the first competent rung it found a development-only Level-2 action-stem
mediator (seed 97) and did not recover a residual-inclusive xor-split
pathway. The post-mortem locates the failure in **intervention
identifiability**, not in step count or objective:

- `G_skip` overwrites residual MSRS members (`hid1`/`hid2` held at A);
- `G_res` holds `skip1 = h0_A` while residual block 1 still reads patched
  `h0_mix`;
- searchable `V = {act_*, b1_*, b2_*}` does not include residual-stream
  coordinates `h0`/`h1`;
- DIRECT xor DISTRIBUTED is therefore not an exclusive edge partition of
  one recovered mechanism.

A successor that only trains longer, retunes 0.50, or switches to JEPA
would not repair that.

## Question (proposed; not frozen)

After a Level-2 mediator for `ax → Δvx` is localized, can **identifiable
edge interventions** resolve the computational route that transmits that
mediator’s counterfactual effect — skip/direct versus residual-branch —
without overwriting the edge under test?

This is **path-identifiability repair** (direction A) plus
**mediator-to-path decomposition** (direction B). Edge-level CRCT
(direction C) is the operational primitive. A subspace ontology
(direction D) is **not** the primary proposal: 004 gauge showed literal
unit instability, not a recovered subspace object.

M = (V, E, I)

V : carrier vs mediator components (do not collapse these)

    skip1: h0 → h1 additive identity
    F1:    h0_branch → residual increment

They are not sufficient as `M`.

## Proposed identifiability requirement (changeable until freeze)

A hold named DIRECT or DISTRIBUTED must be a stated intervention on `E`,
not an alias of “patch `V` then overwrite residual hidden units.”

Minimum semantic repairs to consider at freeze (not yet chosen):

1. Freeze `h0_branch` independently of `skip1`, so residual `F1` can be
   held at A without also freezing the skip, and skip can be held at A
   without feeding `F1` the patched mix.
2. Do not apply residual-unit patches that a subsequent `hid1`/`hid2`
   hold immediately discards; if residual members of `V` are under test,
   the hold must preserve them or the test must be declared
   action-carrier-only.
3. Condition path search on a frozen Level-2 mediator (action-stem or
   residual-inclusive) rather than treating skip-split on `V ⊆ act_*` as
   Level 3.

Until those (or reviewed equivalents) are frozen, `DIRECT` /
`DISTRIBUTED` should not be eligible as a Level-3 status.
