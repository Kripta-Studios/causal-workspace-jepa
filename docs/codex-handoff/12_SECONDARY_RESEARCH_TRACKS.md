# Secondary Research Tracks

## Steerling-inspired ideas — high relevance, low immediate compute

Adopt concepts, not claims:
- known/unknown/residual decomposition;
- coalition-aware circuits;
- functional equivalence;
- signed contribution accounting;
- training-support validity for interventions;
- interpretable-by-design positive controls.

Do not infer that exact `known + unknown + residual = hidden_state` reconstruction proves causal
correctness. Algebraic completeness and causal mechanism validity are different.

Do not run Steerling-8B locally if its practical VRAM requirement exceeds available hardware.
Record `BLOCKED_RESOURCE` rather than forcing quantization into a scientific comparison unless a
separate protocol studies quantization effects.
